Professional Profile

Field Precision & High-Stakes Engineering Site Leadership

I am Mohamed Ali Ahmed Khattab, a 4th-year Mechatronics Engineering student at the Higher Institute of Engineering & Technology in El-Mahalla El-Kubra. Military status: permanently exempt. Since 2022, I have immersed myself in heavy electromechanical field execution across critical national infrastructure projects.

Having served on-site with industry titan REDCON Construction on the landmark 'Future of Egypt' mega project (El Dabaa Corridor), I directed the supervision, installation, alignment, and testing of 14 Japanese Torishima heavy-duty water pumps, along with 11/22 kV medium voltage networks, transformers, motor control centers (VSDs & soft starters), and comprehensive HVAC and firefighting systems.

I excel in uniting multi-stakeholder workflows: managing tight coordination between international equipment manufacturers, technical consultants, and site teams. My focus is delivering uncompromised quality through meticulous IR/MIR submittals and streamlined testing & commissioning.
